# STORIES.md — "The Day in Full", Thursday 19 November 2026

Every item below was verified against a source published on Thursday 19 November 2026
(or reporting an event scheduled/occurring that day). Verification was done on the
morning of 19 Nov (WIB), while the day was still unfolding.

## Panels (1–13)

1. **Shenzhen — first US–China–Russia trilateral.** Trump, Xi and Putin hold the
   first-ever three-way meeting at Kylin Villa, Shenzhen (trilateral 15:30 KST,
   after a 12:45 Trump–Xi bilateral). Washington puts nuclear arms control on the
   table; the Kremlin says Ukraine leads its Trump talks. Agendas conflict, so the
   panel attributes both.
   — AJP (posted 19 Nov 08:59 KST); Reuters, 19 Nov ("Trump caps Asia trip with
   unprecedented meeting with China's Xi and Russia's Putin in Shenzhen").
2. **GTA VI launch day.** Released Thursday 19 Nov at midnight local time, New
   Zealand first; Metacritic 99/100 from 140+ reviews; ~2.3m Twitch viewers by
   12:15 Paris time; $79.99, PS5/Xbox Series only, no PC version.
   — Le Monde, 19 Nov; People (Metacritic tally, 19 Nov); Microsoft/Jagran live blog.
3. **Anthropic's record IPO debuts.** First trades Thursday under ticker ANT;
   priced Wednesday (US time) at $83, above the $70–$78 range; 980m shares raised
   $81.3bn at a ~$1.5tn fully diluted value — the largest IPO on record.
   — Reuters via PitchBook, BusinessWorld and CNN, all timestamped 19 Nov.
4. **ICJ verdict day, The Gambia v. Myanmar.** Judgment reading scheduled Thursday
   10:00 in The Hague (16:00 WIB) — NOT yet delivered at build time, so the panel
   claims no outcome. Case filed 11 Nov 2019, OIC-backed; the 2017 campaign drove
   730,000+ Rohingya into Bangladesh; ICJ judgments are final, no appeal.
   — ICJ schedule and Thursday case coverage.
5. **COP31 Antalya, final stretch.** Summit closes Friday 20 Nov; 21 issues still
   unresolved; new draft texts on adaptation, finance, mitigation and just
   transition due early Thursday; finance text stalled in brackets.
   — Axo News, 18 Nov summit reporting; The News / Reuters, 19 Nov.
6. **Cyclone Akvan nears Odisha.** Rain begins Thursday; schools shut in six
   districts for three days from Thursday; ~50,000 evacuated; IMD landfall expected
   Friday ~noon between Narsapur and Puri, winds 90–100 gusting 110 km/h; red
   warning for 11 districts for 19–20 Nov.
   — Daily Jagran live tracker, published 19 Nov 07:25 IST, citing IMD.
7. **Ukraine's allies meet in Berlin.** "Coalition of the Willing" (Germany,
   France, Britain, Poland, Italy, NATO's Rutte, plus US envoys Witkoff and
   Kushner) meets Thursday to affirm legally binding security guarantees after a
   ceasefire, fearing Shenzhen could settle the war's endgame without Europe.
   — Reuters, 19 Nov.
8. **Xi chairs the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen.** Thursday morning, Xi
   announced US$20m for APEC cooperation and an Asia-Pacific Community Research
   Center in Shenzhen. (Adoption of the Shenzhen Declaration was NOT confirmed —
   not claimed.)
   — Xinhua / AJP, 19 Nov.
9. **Florida flooding closes schools.** Schools closed Thursday across Broward,
   Miami-Dade, Brevard and Volusia counties (Volusia also Friday); flood watch
   through Thursday evening; up to 15 inches of rain fell on Hollywood.
   — CNN live, published 19 Nov 01:35 UTC; Florida Today live updates; NWS via
   People / Miami Herald.
10. **Riyadh missile threat.** The Saudi-led coalition said Thursday it had dealt
    with a Houthi missile threat toward Riyadh; a loud boom was heard, debris fell,
    material damage reported and being assessed; Civil Defense said the danger had
    passed. Shortly before, SPA reported Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met US
    CENTCOM chief Adm. Brad Cooper in Riyadh.
    — Reuters, 19 Nov.
11. **UNHCR halts refugee registration in Malaysia.** The UN agency stops
    registering asylum seekers as Malaysia builds its own asylum system; most
    UNHCR-registered people in Malaysia are Rohingya from Myanmar.
    — Reuters, Thursday 19 Nov 01:58 UTC.
12. **Japan's PM Takaichi talks with Putin at APEC.** Tokyo announced Thursday
    that the sideline talks (following Wednesday's welcome dinner) covered a
    detained Japanese national and protection of Japanese companies.
    — Reuters, 19 Nov (Chief Cabinet Secretary Kihara's announcement).
13. **Kenya floods kill six.** Thursday's Daily Nation reports six dead and
    hundreds displaced as heavy rain floods homes; the Met office warns five
    counties; the Red Cross reports 300 households displaced in Tana North.
    — Daily Nation, Thursday 19 Nov edition; Kenya Met via ronnytv.
